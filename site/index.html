Study: local, secure, reliable agents for engineering design

Human-in-the-loop CAD change agents, measured locally

Which agent architecture can safely change aircraft structural parts from natural-language engineering change requests, using only local LLMs? Three designs compared on 36 change requests and 24 attacks, with every proposal gated by a reviewer. All numbers below are measured on one (no cloud).

Limits

  • The reviewer in the evaluation is a scripted oracle that knows the acceptance criteria. Real reviewers are slower and miss things; review counts are a lower bound on effort.
  • Parts are simplified parametric models with a small rule set (thickness, e/D, pitch, ligament, mass, envelope). No FEA, no certification rules.
  • Attack side effects with defences off are recorded by instrumented stubs and a host safety net; "success" means the agent made the call, not that the host was changed.
  • Temperature 0: seeds change little. Rates come with Wilson 95% intervals, small n per cell.
  • Prompt-level defences (spotlighting, instruction hierarchy) were not tested; only execution-layer defences were.
